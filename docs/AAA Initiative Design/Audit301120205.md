## Executive Summary

This comprehensive audit of the ExxerCube.Prisma codebase confirms an exceptionally strong architectural foundation based on modern C#, DDD, and Hexagonal principles. The project structure, testing strategy, and separation of concerns are professional-grade and built for long-term success.

However, the audit has identified **6 major findings**, including **2 CRITICAL MVP blockers**. While many high-level services and interfaces exist, their underlying implementations are either missing or dependent on placeholder domain models. The most critical gap is the absence of a complete core domain model and the event handler to orchestrate the processing pipeline. Without these, the system is fundamentally non-functional despite its excellent architecture.

The current funding recommendation is **AT RISK**. The codebase demonstrates superior architectural vision but lacks the implemented business logic to prove its value proposition. To secure funding, the immediate focus must be on implementing the critical and urgent findings outlined in this report, specifically building the domain model and connecting the processing pipeline to deliver a demonstrable, end-to-end vertical slice.

#### Finding C-2: Missing Pipeline Orchestration Event Handler

**Status**: CONFIRMED

**Requirement Sources**:

**Gap Definition**:

- [x] DI registration: **missing**
