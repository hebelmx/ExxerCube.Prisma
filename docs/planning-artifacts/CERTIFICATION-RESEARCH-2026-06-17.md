# Veriqan Regulatory-Positioning Research — CONDUSEF Statement-Format Compliance

> Background research for the Veriqan compliance-verification product. Verdict drives the
> coverage-deck positioning and Epic 12 (traceability/personalization).

## Verdict (high confidence)

**No certification exists.** There is no CONDUSEF / CNBV / Banxico certification, accreditation,
*padrón*, or approved-vendor scheme for compliance with the standardized credit-card statement
*Acuerdo* (DOF 29-Dec-2022, mandatory 17-Oct-2024) — neither for the banks' statements nor for
third-party verification software. **No competitor was found** doing CONDUSEF statement-format
verification (RegTech players surfaced only in adjacent PLD/AML/KYC domains, e.g. Regcheq).

## How compliance is actually enforced (the market reality)

- **CONDUSEF is the substantive authority** (LTOSF art. 13 statement-format power; LPDUSF sanction
  power). It runs an annual **"Evaluación/Supervisión del Producto Tarjeta de Crédito"** that pulls
  client *expedientes* and checks the Contrato, Carátula, **Estado de Cuenta**, publicidad, web —
  scoring each institution and publishing results via **"El Calificador"** and the **Buró de
  Entidades Financieras**. Deficiencies → **Programa de Cumplimiento Forzoso** + fines.
- **CNBV** performs on-site inspection only **at CONDUSEF's request**.
- Mandatory date anchored in the **Disposición en materia de Registros** (DOF 14-Oct-2022, DÉCIMO
  SEXTO TRANSITORIO), scoped to **RECA-registered** credit-card contracts.
- **No statement-specific self-certification or mandatory external audit** exists; banks comply
  reactively. No dedicated despacho-attestation market found.

## Adjacent registries (context, not claims)

RECA (scope anchor — the deadline references it), SIPRES (who is regulated), REUS (do-not-contact),
Buró/El Calificador (the consequence surface), PUR/SINE (the registry umbrella). **None certifies
statement format.** Never imply "certified by CONDUSEF" — it would be false.

## What Veriqan CAN credibly pursue

- **ISO/IEC 27001 + SOC 2 Type II** — the real, bank-recognized credentials a RegTech can earn.
- **Design to the CNBV Circular Única de Bancos (CUB) outsourcing / operational-tech-risk regime** —
  if a bank embeds Veriqan, the bank's CUB obligations flow down (audit rights, infosec annex, data
  confidentiality, business continuity, ≥2-yearly third-party audit).

## Positioning (drives the deck)

1. **Sell traceability, not a badge** — every check anchored to the exact DOF *Acuerdo* numeral +
   *Guía de Llenado* field, plus Banxico **Circular 13/2011** (pago mínimo) and **Circular 34/2010
   §3.4** (cargos no reconocidos). Output = an auditable numeral-by-numeral map a bank can hand to
   CONDUSEF in a Programa de Cumplimiento Forzoso. → **This is Epic 12 + NFR-7.**
2. **Pre-emptive self-supervision** — frame as passing "El Calificador" / avoiding fines; quantify
   ROI (fine avoidance + Buró score/reputation).
3. **First mover** — no competitor found; the regime is granular, mandatory, annually graded & fined.
